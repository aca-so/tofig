Why tofig
by acaso
Built for real handoff.
Editable layers
Not a screenshot — real frames, text and vectors you can edit.
Design + Slides
Frames in Design files, slides in Figma Slides — automatic.
100% local
Runs entirely in the plugin. Your HTML never leaves Figma.
Font-aware
Maps type to your Figma fonts and reports what was substituted.

Inline SVG → editable vectors. CSS gradients → native fills.
